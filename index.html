Kira GTA6

Kira GTA6 is a private web dashboard used by a single creator to prepare short news videos about the video game GTA 6 and publish them to the creator's own TikTok account.

The creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, reviews every finished video in the dashboard, and publishes it only by clicking "Send to TikTok". Nothing is posted automatically. The dashboard runs privately on the creator's own computer and is not open to the public.
